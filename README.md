# GAIL Energy Connect

**Jodo Pipeline. Jagao Shehar.**  
A playable PWA micro-game for GAIL’s *Wah Kya Energy Hai* social campaign.

Connect sections of the JHBDPL / Pradhan Mantri Urja Ganga corridor, watch CGD cities come alive, and share a personalised energy badge.

## Play

**[Play GAIL Energy Connect](https://rajeshkrj-arch.github.io/gail/)**

Open it on a phone, tap **Start connecting**, and add it to the home screen. No app store.

GitHub Pages hosts the playable game. The weekly leaderboard needs the Grok/Vercel deploy (server database); scores still save on the device.

## Loop

**Connect → Energize → Activate → Score → Share**

1. Drag (or tap) the matching pipe onto the glowing joint.
2. An energy pulse runs the line.
3. The city transforms — Varanasi, Patna, Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Kolkata each have their own life.
4. Energy points and the impact meter fill.
5. Share a *Wah Kya Energy Hai* card.

Wrong pieces are not punished harshly: **Check the route.**

## Factual backbone

JHBDPL supplies gas to CGD networks along the eastern corridor, including Varanasi, Patna, Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Bhubaneswar, Cuttack and Kolkata. The in-game map is stylised for mobile play, not a GIS plot.

## Stack

HTML5 PWA · React · SVG map · local scoring · weekly leaderboard (alias, no forced login)
